Dr. Brian Huber

MD
Dermatology Hair & Scalp Disorders Trichology

Dr. Brian Huber is a board-certified dermatologist whose clinical and academic focus is hair and scalp disorders, including the medical and surgical management of androgenetic alopecia and hair restoration. His practice spans the full arc of hair loss care — from diagnosis and pharmacological management to peri-operative planning around transplantation and the regimens that determine long-term outcomes. He has a particular interest in the gap between what hair-growth products claim and what the trial literature actually supports, and contributes evidence-based reviews to help readers separate genuine mechanistic innovation from cosmeticized marketing.
