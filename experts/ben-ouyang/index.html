Dr. Ben Ouyang

Dr. Ben Ouyang is a physician-scientist with an MD and PhD, affiliated with Harvard. His work spans cancer biology, clinical oncology, and the translation of laboratory findings into clinical practice. He has a particular interest in how metabolic mechanisms are interpreted — and frequently misinterpreted — in patient-facing wellness contexts. He contributes evidence-based analysis to help readers understand what the clinical trial literature actually supports.
