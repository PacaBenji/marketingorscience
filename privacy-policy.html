Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow us to recognize your browser and remember certain information about your visit, such as your preferences and how you navigate the site.

How We Use Cookies

Marketing or Science uses cookies primarily to understand our readership and improve the editorial experience. Specifically, we use cookies to:

  • Identify returning visitors and distinguish between new and existing readers
  • Understand which articles, topics, and sections are most popular
  • Analyze traffic patterns and reader behavior across our site
  • Measure the effectiveness of our content and editorial decisions
  • Maintain your preferences (such as display settings or saved articles)

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies are required for the site to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas.

Analytics cookies help us understand how readers interact with our content. We collect aggregated, anonymized data about page views, session duration, and referral sources.

Functional cookies remember choices you make — such as your region or language preference — to provide a more personalized experience.

Third-party cookies may be set by embedded content (e.g., social media share buttons, video players, or comment sections) from external providers. These are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties.

Reader Identification

To understand who is reading our content, we may use cookies in combination with account registration data (if applicable) or anonymized device identifiers. We do not use cookies to identify you personally without your consent, and any data collected for reader analytics is aggregated or pseudonymized wherever possible.

Your Choices

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.
  • Consent banner: When you first visit our site, you will be asked to accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics, you may use your browser's built-in privacy controls or privacy-focused browser extensions to prevent data collection.

Data Retention

Cookie data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, typically no longer than 12 months. Analytics data may be retained in aggregated form for longer periods.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on the site or updating the date at the top of this section.
